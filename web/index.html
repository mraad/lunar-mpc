What am I looking at—and what does it prove?

This app plays actual recorded runs from the Python Adaptive MPC controller. Changing a scenario or controller loads a different recorded experiment; it does not run a new optimization in your browser. The dashed future is the plan made at that exact recorded step.

The adaptive controller estimates engine effectiveness from observed motion using recursive least squares. The fixed-model version still replans after every observation, but keeps its original physics parameters. A 30% fault means the simulator reduces main-engine power; that fault setting is never passed to the controller.

In a historical 50-seed evaluation before standalone extraction, adaptive MPC passed 49/50 terminal checks with the fault, compared with 42/50 for the fixed model. Both passed 49/50 normally. The eight starts shown here were development cases. There is a known viewport-recovery failure, approximate contact modeling, and occasional planning times over 20 ms. This is a research demonstration, with no certified safety or real-time guarantee.
